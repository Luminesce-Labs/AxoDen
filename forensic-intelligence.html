Forensic intelligence

Prototype

The prototype ingests evidence and constructs and analyses graphs linking events, processes, systems and investigative claims. Selected kernel controls govern admission and recorded outcomes.

Testing has included Mordor adversary-emulation telemetry and confidential alert data supplied under non-disclosure agreements. These are distinct test sources. Operational deployment requires an evaluation of this application on its own.
