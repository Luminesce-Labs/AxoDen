# Evidence systems for high-stakes decisions

AxoDen helps teams inspect, govern, and replay the evidence behind complex decisions.

We work where ordinary checklists are not enough: regulated dossiers, AI assurance, forensic evidence, RF-to-AI systems, crisis operations, and technical discovery workflows.

Our systems model evidence as structured graphs. That lets us detect missing evidence, contradictions, stale signals, brittle dependencies, common-mode risk, and proxy gaming before they become operational failures.

## What We Do

AxoDen builds evidence-assurance systems for organisations that need decisions to be traceable, auditable, and resistant to failure under pressure.

Our work combines:

- structured evidence graphs
- deterministic admission and refusal gates
- contradiction and gap detection
- independence and common-mode risk monitoring
- Goodhart and proxy-drift detection
- replayable ledgers for audit and review

The result is not a generic AI assistant. It is an evidence-control layer for decisions where provenance, completeness, and accountability matter.

## How We Work

### 1. Frame the Evidence System

We define the decision, the governing schema, the evidence sources, and the action boundary.

### 2. Build the Evidence Graph

We convert documents, claims, observations, controls, and provenance into a structured graph that can be inspected and replayed.

### 3. Run Capability Checks

We check for missing evidence, contradictions, stale records, common-mode dependence, brittleness, proxy drift, and admission failures.

### 4. Produce a Pilot Output

We return a focused pilot report, ranked findings, and a practical implementation path.

### 5. Integrate Where Useful

If the pilot is valuable, we help turn it into a repeatable workflow, API, dashboard, or audit process.

## Why AxoDen

Most evidence workflows ask what is present.

AxoDen also asks:

- What should be present but is missing?
- Which evidence contradicts other evidence?
- Which records have lost freshness or provenance?
- Which assumptions carry too much structural load?
- Which monitors are not independent?
- Which metrics are being optimised while their evidence base decays?
- Which decisions should be refused, throttled, or escalated?

That makes AxoDen useful where a faster checklist is not enough.
