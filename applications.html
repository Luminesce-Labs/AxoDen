Three applications, three evaluation states.

AxoDen domain applications

Supplier documents, security events and RF-derived inputs mean different things in their own domains. Support, dependence, provenance, contradiction and freshness recur across them.

Dossier Intelligence is the commercial workflow. Forensic intelligence and Spectrum Bridge are further settings in which to evaluate reuse. On this page, pilot-stage means internal development short of production. That is a separate state from a contracted customer pilot.

Dossier Intelligence

Supplier-evidence review for aerospace and defence. The MVP is in expert evaluation by four subject-matter experts. A contract and a formal pilot remain to be established.

Forensic intelligence

A prototype that connects security events into inspectable evidence for analysts. Operational deployment needs its own evaluation.

Spectrum Bridge

Pilot-stage middleware between RF-derived inputs and an onboard edge AI computer. Physical calibration and the full action path remain open.

Forensic intelligence

The prototype ingests evidence and constructs and analyses graphs linking events, processes, systems and investigative claims. Selected kernel controls govern admission and recorded outcomes.

Testing has included Mordor adversary-emulation telemetry and confidential alert data supplied under non-disclosure agreements. These are distinct test sources. The work shows the evidence-analysis approach inside that recorded testing scope.

Operational deployment and customer benefit require an evaluation of this application on its own.

Recorded scope

  • Domain ingestion, graph construction and analysis.
  • Admission and recorded outcomes under selected kernel controls.
  • Mordor adversary-emulation telemetry.
  • A separate set of confidential alert data under NDA. The provider is unnamed here.

Spectrum Bridge

Spectrum Bridge is pilot-stage assurance middleware between RF-derived inputs and an onboard edge AI computer.

It applies declared admission and refusal rules to control what reaches the AI, and records the inputs, checks and decisions in an append-only evidence ledger for audit and replay. Session-bound controls address the risk of recovered communications or persisted instructions being treated as sufficient current authority.

Recorded local verification covers replay and shared-kernel compatibility within a defined host, profile and fixture scope. Calibrated physical performance, operational session management and enforcement through the complete action path remain integration and evaluation work.

What this page covers

  • Admission and refusal in front of the AI.
  • An append-only ledger for audit and replay.
  • Session-bound checks on current authority.
  • Local verification inside a defined host, profile and fixture scope.

What comes next

The priorities are a defined Dossier trial, broader operator coverage, application integration and composed assurance-architecture validation, including the outstanding evaluator-independence and data-provenance obligations.
