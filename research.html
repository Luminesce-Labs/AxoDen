One scoped machine-checked result

The classical global composition theorem for Entropy-Bounded Data Pipelines has a machine-checked proof in Lean 4 with Mathlib, for finite discrete variables. It establishes the stated composition bound inside that scope.

Entropy-Frugal Intelligence composition remains an analytical argument under functional-stage and model-state independence assumptions. Other results in the programme are formal models, analytical arguments or computational investigations. Those forms of evidence support different claims.

Shared model

The graph-based contributions share the AxoDen Evidence Graph. Geometry of Epistemic Integrity treats reasoning structure, live assumptions and knowledge over time as connected views of that graph.

The Attested Epistemic Integrity Plane specifies how independence, attestation, reasoning structure, live assumptions and proxy validity come together around a decision. Implementation of the complete runtime plane remains outstanding.

APASES is the research environment

APASES connects task routing, source retrieval, methodology selection, tool access, computation, counterexample search, verification and claim records.

It is the environment used to do the work, from problem framing through investigation to modelling, experiments and software. On this site it appears only in that role: an internal research system.

How the record is used

A theorem, paper or benchmark can support a pilot hypothesis. It still has to be translated into a scoped question, a dataset, an acceptance test and a decision about weak evidence.

Commercial use checks the claim against the system in front of the buyer. The research record does not replace that check.
