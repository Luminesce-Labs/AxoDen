Spectrum Bridge

Pilot-stage RF-to-AI middleware

Spectrum Bridge is pilot-stage assurance middleware between RF-derived inputs and an onboard edge AI computer. It applies declared admission and refusal rules, and records the inputs, checks and decisions in an append-only evidence ledger.

Recorded local verification covers replay and shared-kernel compatibility within a defined host, profile and fixture scope. Calibrated physical performance, operational session management and enforcement through the complete action path remain integration and evaluation work.
